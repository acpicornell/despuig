## Scope

- This license covers the **data** only.
- The underlying map image is a historical work; the high-resolution master used
  here comes from the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa (CC BY 4.0).
- Canonical toponym forms come from the **NGIB** (Nomenclàtor Geogràfic de les
  Illes Balears) and remain subject to their own terms.
- The pipeline **code** in this repository is not covered by this data license.
